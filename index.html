Wonjun Jo

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Electrical Engineering at POSTECH, advised by Prof. Tae-Hyun Oh at KAIST’s AMI Lab. I also conduct research with the Physical AI group at KIST, advised by Prof. In So Kweon.

My research focuses on embodied AI and robot learning, building on self-supervised and multimodal learning. Recently, I have been studying how robots can adapt to new tasks and acquire reusable skills, focusing on vision-language-action (VLA) model adaptation and agentic robot learning.
